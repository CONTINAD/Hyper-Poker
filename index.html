Three steps. Zero trust.

  1. 01
    Commit before deal

    Server publishes a SHA-256 hash of the seed. Anyone can record it.

  2. 02
    Mix in your entropy

    Each seat contributes a random nonce. The deck is keyed by HMAC of the combined input.

  3. 03
    Reveal & verify

    After the river, the seed is published. Re-derive the deck yourself in two lines of code.
